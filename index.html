How I make systems better

Most operations already have a system. It lives in spreadsheets, WhatsApp threads and one person's head. My job is to find where it breaks, fix that first, and make the fix permanent.

Map the real process first

Before writing any code I trace how requests, data and decisions actually move, and who touches them. The gaps show up quickly.

At HedgeGuard, scattered trackers became one Operations Hub, with each list surfacing in the Teams channel where the work happens.

Automate what repeats

Any check done by hand every day is a candidate. I track how often a workaround gets used and turn the frequent ones into scheduled, monitored automations.

Daily NAV verification across client instances is moving from manual checks to scheduled pipelines with alerting.

Ship in phases people can use

A working pilot in weeks beats a perfect plan in months. Each phase is live, measured and owned by the client before the next begins.

The AACI ERP started as one hub and grew to three: projects, finance and HR. It's in daily use.

          How a project runs

          Phased delivery. You see working software early, give feedback on real screens, and decide what comes next based on what people actually use.

          1. Discovery

            Time with the people who do the work. Priorities, constraints, systems to connect.

            1–2 weeks
          2. Prototype

            Key screens and flows you can click through and put in front of the people who will use them.

            2–3 weeks
          3. Pilot

            Core features live with a first group of users, on real data.

            6–10 weeks
          4. Measure

            Usage, time saved, errors caught. We fix what the numbers show.

            Ongoing
          5. Next phase

            Roll out to more teams or sites and add the next set of features, priced separately.
